⏺ Here's the honest answer to your question, from testing it just now:

  Lean gives no live progress inside a file. Messages are buffered and dumped
  when the module finishes — I verified this by putting a 5-second IO.sleep
  between two #print axioms markers and running lean --json: both messages
  arrived at the same timestamp. So --json, -v, and marker commands all get you
  nothing until the end.

  What does work — three things, in order of usefulness:

  1. One theorem per module. lake build prints a line per module as it
     completes, so you get per-dispatcher progress and per-dispatcher caching —
     a dispatcher that succeeds stays built, so a fix only recompiles the one
     that failed. This is exactly why RubyCore/Proof/KontFrameDispatch.lean is
     split from KontFrame.lean. I should have done this from the start.
  2. set_option profiler true — per-declaration and per-tactic timings, but only
     in the final dump. Post-hoc, not live.

     tells you whether the run is healthy, and here it was decisive:
