  The proof is what validates the fix

  capStale — the function Judge.vasgn uses to decide which bindings to widen —
  turned out to be the side condition of denM_setLocal, the transport of a

  one predicate. A fix that had merely been sufficient to reject the 
  counterexample would have surfaced here as a transport needing some other
  hypothesis.

  Why the twin and not vasgn itself: vasgn's right-hand side is an arbitrary
  expression, so it runs under a pushed continuation while its premise is about
  a run under the empty one — the continuation-decomposition wall that gates
  every compound rung. vasgnAlias's RHS is a .var, so its whole run is four
  concrete stepFn steps. That split is the sharpest statement available: the 
  rule is sound and provably so; the general one waits on a machine lemma, not 
  on assignment.
